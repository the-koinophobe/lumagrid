Farms around **Ughelli, Abraka, Agbor, Sapele and Asaba** keep birds alive with steady power. Generators are costly and unreliable. Solar is a good fit for farms.

## What a poultry farm runs

<div className="table-scroll">

| Load | Notes |
|---|---|
| LED house lighting | Many hours a day |
| Ventilation fans | Daytime heat |
| Water pump | Daytime, fill tanks |
| Incubator | 24 hours, critical |
| Freezer | For processed birds |
| Security lights / CCTV | Night |

</div>

## Why farms suit solar

- **Space.** Panels can go on the ground or pen roofs. Read [roof vs ground mount](/blog/roof-mount-vs-ground-mount-solar).
- **Day loads.** Fans and pumps run when the sun is strongest.

## What to put on the battery

- Incubators
- Night lighting
- Freezers
- Security

## What not to run on solar

Electric heaters for brooding use lots of power. Gas or charcoal brooders are cheaper to run.

## Water

Pump into tanks in the day. Read [solar borehole vs generator pump](/blog/solar-borehole-vs-generator-pump).

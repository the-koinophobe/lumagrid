Communities along the creeks of **Rivers** (Bonny, Okrika, Degema, Abonnema, Kalabari towns) and **Bayelsa** (Brass, Nembe, Southern Ijaw, Ekeremor) often have no grid at all. Fuel comes by boat and costs more. Solar changes daily life.

## Why solar saves more here

- Petrol and diesel cost more after boat transport.
- Grid supply is weak or absent.
- Generators break and parts are far away.

## How we plan a riverine install

1. **Load list** over WhatsApp, with photos of the house.
2. **Design** for off-grid, with extra panels and battery.
3. **Pack and check** every part before leaving Port Harcourt or Yenagoa.
4. **One trip** install where possible.
5. **Logistics cost** shown on the quote.

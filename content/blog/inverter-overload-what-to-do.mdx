The inverter beeps. Then it shuts off. The screen says **overload**. Here is why.

## Two kinds of overload

### 1. Too much at once

Iron (1,200W) + kettle (2,000W) + fridge + fans on a 3kVA inverter = too much.

### 2. The start-up surge

Motors need a big burst to start. A normal AC can pull several times its running power for a second. Pumps and fridges do it too.

## Heavy items to watch

<div className="table-scroll">

| Appliance | Watts |
|---|---|
| Electric kettle | 1,500–2,200W |
| Pressing iron | 1,000–1,200W |
| Microwave | 1,000–1,500W |
| Hair dryer | 1,000–2,000W |
| 1.5HP AC (running) | 1,100–1,500W |
| 1HP pump (running) | ~750W, big surge |

</div>

## Simple habits

1. **One heavy item at a time.**
2. **Start the AC or pump first**, then other loads.
3. Use **inverter ACs**. Read [inverter AC vs normal AC](/blog/inverter-ac-vs-normal-ac-on-solar).
4. Iron and pump **in the daytime**.

## Fixes

- A **soft starter** for pumps
- A **bigger inverter** if overload is daily
- Moving some loads to a separate circuit

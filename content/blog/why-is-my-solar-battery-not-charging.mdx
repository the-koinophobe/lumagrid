The sun is out. But your battery is not filling. Here is how to find out why.

## Step 1: Look at the PV number

Your inverter screen shows **PV** (solar) watts.

<div className="table-scroll">

| PV reading in daylight | Points to |
|---|---|
| 0W | Breaker, isolator, cable or panel fault |
| Low | Clouds, dirt, shade |
| Good, but battery not rising | Loads, settings or battery problem |

</div>

## The 8 common causes

1. **Cloudy or rainy day.** Normal. Read [solar in rainy season](/blog/solar-panel-maintenance-rainy-season).
2. **Dirty panels.** Soot and dust. Read [how to clean panels](/blog/how-to-clean-solar-panels-safely).
3. **New shade.** A tree, tank or new building.
4. **Tripped DC breaker or isolator.**
5. **Loose or burnt connector.**
6. **Wrong settings** after a reset or change.
7. **Daytime loads** using all the solar (AC, pump, iron).
8. **BMS protection or ageing battery.**

## What you can safely do

- Check the weather.
- Look for dirt or shade.
- Switch off heavy daytime loads and watch the battery %.
- Check whether a breaker has tripped (do not open sealed DC boxes).

## When to call us

- PV shows 0W on a sunny day
- Burning smell or melted connectors
- BMS or fault codes that will not clear

Frozen fish, chicken and meat sellers in **Port Harcourt, Onne, Eleme, Bonny and Borokiri** lose money fast when power fails. Solar can help, but cold storage is serious work.

## Why cold storage is hard

- It runs **24 hours a day**.
- The compressor is a big motor with a start-up surge.
- Every door opening lets warm air in.

## The hybrid design for cold rooms

1. **Day:** panels run the cold room and charge the batteries.
2. **Night:** batteries run it.
3. **Cloudy days:** grid or generator tops up.

## Cut the load first

- Thick insulation and good door seals
- Strip curtains at the door
- Load and unload quickly
- Keep it full

These can cut power use a lot, and that makes the solar system smaller.

AC is the biggest load most homes in Port Harcourt want on solar. The type of AC you have changes everything.

## Two kinds of AC

- **Normal AC** (non-inverter): runs at full power, then stops, then starts again. Every start is a big kick.
- **Inverter AC**: starts softly, runs fast to cool the room, then slows down and keeps the room cool with less power.

## Side by side (1.5HP)

<div className="table-scroll">

| | Normal AC | Inverter AC |
|---|---|---|
| Start-up surge | Very big | Small |
| Running power | About 1.1 to 1.5kW, on/off | Varies, often 0.7 to 0.9kW average |
| Energy for 8 hours | About 9 to 11kWh | About 6 to 8kWh |
| Inverter size needed | Bigger | 5kVA can handle one |
| Battery needed | More | Less |

</div>

## Why the start-up kick matters

When a normal AC starts, it can pull several times its running power for a second or two. A small solar inverter may trip. That is why a 3kVA system often fails with a normal AC.

## Why the night use matters

Your battery runs the AC at night. If your AC uses 10kWh instead of 7kWh, you need about 3kWh more battery. At roughly ₦220,000 per kWh, that is a big difference.

## What to do

1. If you are buying a new AC, buy **inverter** type.
2. Set it to 24–25°C, not 16°C.
3. Close doors and windows. Use curtains.
4. Clean the filters every month. Dirty filters waste power.

## Local tip

In GRA Port Harcourt and Asaba duplexes, we often see two old normal ACs. Swapping them for inverter ACs can cut the battery you need by a lot. That can pay for the new ACs.

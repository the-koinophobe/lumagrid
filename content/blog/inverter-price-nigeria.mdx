## Must-have features

- **Pure sine wave.** Read [pure vs modified](/blog/pure-sine-wave-vs-modified-sine-wave).
- **Built-in MPPT.** Read [MPPT vs PWM](/blog/mppt-vs-pwm-charge-controller).
- **Lithium support.**

## Nice-to-have features

- Battery **communication** (the battery talks to the inverter)
- **Wi-Fi monitoring** on your phone. Read [solar monitoring apps](/blog/solar-monitoring-app-explained).
- **Parallel** capability to add a second inverter later
- **Two MPPT inputs** for panels on two roof faces

## Watch out for

- "5kVA" units that cannot actually deliver 4kW+
- No local warranty support
- Very old used units. Read [new vs used inverters](/blog/new-vs-used-inverter-nigeria).

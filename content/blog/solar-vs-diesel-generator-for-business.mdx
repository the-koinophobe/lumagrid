Diesel generators keep many businesses in Port Harcourt, Warri and Onne running. But they are expensive to feed. Let us compare.

## How to work out your diesel cost

Use this simple formula:

```
Litres per hour × Hours per day × Price per litre × 30 days
```

**Example:** a generator uses 2 litres an hour, runs 10 hours a day. Say diesel is ₦1,300 a litre.

```
2 × 10 × 1,300 × 30 = ₦780,000 a month
```

Then add oil changes, filters, repairs and the time the generator is down.

## The hidden costs of diesel

- **Fuel scarcity.** When fuel is short, you queue or close.
- **Theft.** Diesel and batteries get stolen.
- **Downtime.** A broken generator means lost sales.
- **Soot.** In Port Harcourt, soot is already a problem. Generators add to it.

## What solar does for a business

- Power is there when the sun is up, which is when most shops are busy.
- Batteries carry the evening.
- A hybrid inverter can use grid power when PHED or BEDC supply comes.
- The generator becomes backup only. It runs for an hour, not all day.

## Which businesses gain the most

- Supermarkets and mini-marts with freezers
- Pharmacies with fridges
- Offices with computers and ACs
- Cold rooms and fish sellers
- Hotels and guest houses

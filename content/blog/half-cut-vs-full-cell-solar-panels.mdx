Look closely at a new solar panel. You may see a line across the middle. The cells look like rectangles. That is a **half-cut** panel.

## What changes when cells are cut in half

- Each half carries **less current**.
- Less current means **less heat** inside the panel.
- The panel is wired in **two halves**.

## Why this helps on Nigerian roofs

Roofs in Port Harcourt, Warri and Yenagoa often have:

- Overhead water tanks
- Parapet walls
- Satellite dishes
- Trees and taller neighbours

These cast shadows. On a full-cell panel, a small shadow can drag down a big part of the panel. On a half-cut panel, the shaded half drops, but the other half keeps working.

## Shade still matters

Half-cut panels help, but they cannot beat heavy shade. Good design comes first. During a site visit, we check where shadows fall in the morning and afternoon. Read about [solar on upstairs buildings](/blog/solar-on-upstairs-building), where shade from tanks and parapets is common.

## What to look for

1. A line across the middle of the panel
2. 120, 132 or 144 cells on the datasheet
3. A trusted brand and a 25-year warranty

In **Warri, Effurun, Asaba, Sapele and Ughelli**, light from BEDC comes and goes. When it comes, it is sometimes too weak to use.

## Two problems

1. **No light** (outages)
2. **Weak light** (low voltage): bulbs dim, fans slow, fridge will not start

## Common causes

- Limited supply to the network
- Overloaded transformers
- Faulty lines and joints
- Vandalism
- Storms in the rainy season

## What to do

1. **Report it** to BEDC through official channels, with your meter or account number.
2. **Protect appliances** with stabilisers and surge protection.
3. **Do not run motors on very low voltage.** It burns them.

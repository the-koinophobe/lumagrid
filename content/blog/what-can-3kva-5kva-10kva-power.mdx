Inverters are sold by kVA, batteries by kWh, and most people are told a size without being told what it actually runs. This guide fixes that.

## kVA vs kWh in one minute

- **kVA (inverter size)** is how much you can run **at the same time**. A 3kVA inverter delivers roughly 2.4kW to 3kW continuously, depending on the model.
- **kWh (battery size)** is how **long** you can run it without sun. A 5kWh lithium battery gives you about 4kWh to 4.5kWh of usable energy.

So inverter size decides *what* you can switch on; battery size decides *how long* it lasts at night. Panels decide how fast the battery refills the next day.

**Best for:** one- and two-bedroom flats, shops and offices without AC. With our standard 5kWh lithium battery, a 400W evening load (lights, fans, TV, fridge) runs for about 11 hours.

**Price:** from about ₦2.8M installed.

## What a 5kVA solar system can power

Everything above, plus:

- A bigger fridge **and** a freezer
- A washing machine
- Pressing iron or microwave without switching everything else off
- One 1HP or 1.5HP **inverter** AC

**Best for:** two- and three-bedroom homes, salons, pharmacies and small offices. With a 5kWh battery, a 1.5HP inverter AC plus lights and fans runs for about 3 to 4 hours at night. Running the AC all night needs more storage, which is why most of our AC homes use the 6kVA package with 16kWh.

**Price:** usually ₦2.2M to ₦2.8M with 5kWh of lithium.

## What a 6kVA solar system can power

The sweet spot for a family home with one AC. Our 6kVA package pairs a hybrid inverter with a 16kWh lithium battery and eight 600W panels, enough for a 1.5HP inverter AC through the night plus the fridge, fans, lights and TV.

**Price:** from about ₦4.9M installed.

## What a 10kVA solar system can power

- Two 1.5HP inverter ACs
- A 1HP water pump or borehole (see [solar for borehole pumps](/blog/solar-borehole-water-pump-nigeria))
- Multiple freezers or a small cold room
- A full office: computers, printers, CCTV, POS

**Best for:** duplexes, guest houses, offices, schools and churches. Pair it with 15kWh or more of lithium storage.

**Price:** roughly ₦5.1M to ₦6.5M with 15kWh of lithium.

| 13kWh (15kWh battery) | 40+ hrs | ~21 hrs | ~11 hrs |

These are rough figures. Real backup depends on how your loads cycle on and off; a fridge, for example, does not draw power all the time.

## The mistakes we see most

1. **Buying a big inverter with a tiny battery.** It runs your AC beautifully until 10pm, then everything goes off.
2. **Running an AC on a 3kVA system.** The startup surge trips the inverter. See [can solar power an AC](/blog/solar-ac).
3. **Too few panels.** The battery never fully recharges, especially in rainy season. Read [how many panels a 5kVA inverter needs](/blog/how-many-solar-panels-for-5kva-inverter).

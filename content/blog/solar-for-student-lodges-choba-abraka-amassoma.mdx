University towns have a power problem. **Choba and Alakahia** near UNIPORT. **Abraka** near DELSU. **Amassoma** near NDU. **Otuoke** near FUO. Students study at night. Landlords run generators all evening.

## What students use

<div className="table-scroll">

| Item | Watts |
|---|---|
| Fan | 60W |
| LED bulbs | 20W |
| Phone + power bank | 20W |
| Laptop | 60W |
| **Per room** | **About 160W** |

</div>

12 rooms × 160W = about **1,900W** in the evening.

## The setup

- Panels on the lodge roof
- One central inverter and lithium bank
- A **breaker per room**, sized for light loads
- A lockable battery room

## House rules that protect the system

1. No hot plates or electric cookers.
2. Pressing iron only in the daytime.
3. No room heaters or big speakers at night.

## Sizes

<div className="table-scroll">

| Rooms | Typical system |
|---|---|
| 6 | 5kVA–6kVA, 8kWh+ lithium |
| 12 | 10kVA, 15kWh+ lithium |
| 20+ | 10kVA–15kVA+, 20kWh+ lithium |

</div>

## Security

Lodges are busy places. Lock the battery room. Use anti-theft bolts on panels. Read [solar panel theft prevention](/blog/solar-panel-theft-prevention).

Not everyone can buy a big system at once. That is fine. You can **start small and grow**, if you plan it right.

## Buy these for the future

### 1. The inverter

Inverters are hard to "add to". If you think you will run an AC within a year, start with **5kVA**, not 3kVA. Read [3kVA vs 5kVA](/blog/3kva-vs-5kva-solar-system).

### 2. A 48V system

48V is easy to grow. Read [12V vs 24V vs 48V](/blog/12v-vs-24v-vs-48v-solar-system).

## Add these later

### Batteries

Start with 5kWh. Add another 5kWh later. Use the **same model** and voltage.

### Panels

Add panels up to the inverter's solar limit. Leave roof space. Read [how many panels for 5kVA](/blog/how-many-solar-panels-for-5kva-inverter).

## A sample plan

<div className="table-scroll">

| Stage | What you buy | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 5kVA inverter, 5kWh lithium, 6 panels | Lights, fans, TV, fridge all night |
| Year 2 | +5kWh lithium | AC for part of the night |
| Year 3 | +2–4 panels | Faster charging in rainy season |

## Tips

- Keep your invoice and battery model.
- Tell your installer your future plans.
- Do not mix lithium and lead-acid.

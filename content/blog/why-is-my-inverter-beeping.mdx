Beep. Beep. Beep. Your inverter is trying to tell you something. Here is what it usually means.

## Common reasons

<div className="table-scroll">

| Screen / sign | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Overload icon, load % high | Too many things on | Switch off iron, kettle, AC, pump |
| Battery icon low | Battery nearly empty | Reduce load, let it charge |
| Grid / AC input warning | NEPA voltage too low or high | Wait, or check input settings |
| Temperature icon | Inverter too hot | Improve airflow, clear dust |
| Fault code (e.g. 0x, F-) | Internal or wiring fault | Photo it, call installer |

</div>

## First steps

1. **Read the screen.** Note the code or icon.
2. **Switch off heavy loads.**
3. **Check airflow** around the inverter.
4. **Wait a few minutes.** Many warnings clear.
5. **Still beeping?** Take a photo and send it to your installer.

## Do not

- Open the inverter.
- Keep resetting it again and again.
- Ignore a burning smell. Switch off at the isolator.

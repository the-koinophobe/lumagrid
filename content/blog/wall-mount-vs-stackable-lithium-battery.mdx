Lithium batteries come in different shapes. The shape changes where you can put it and how easy it is to add more later.

## The three main types

<div className="table-scroll">

| | Wall-mount | Stackable / floor-standing | Rack |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where it goes | On the wall | On the floor | In a metal cabinet |
| Space | Saves floor | Uses floor | Uses floor |
| Flood safety | Best | Needs a raised base | Needs a raised base |
| Adding more | Add another unit on wall | Add a module on top | Add a module in the rack |
| Best for | Flats, small homes | Family homes | Offices, big systems |

</div>

## Wall-mount

Great for flats in **Rumuola, Woji** or **Effurun** where space is small. It also keeps the battery high. That matters in flood-prone parts of **Yenagoa** and **Sagbama**. Read [protecting solar from floods](/blog/protect-solar-system-from-flooding-bayelsa).

## Stackable or floor-standing

Modules sit on top of each other. Some have wheels. Easy to grow: add another module when you need more power. Put it on a raised base if your area floods.

## Rack

Many modules slide into a metal rack. Common for offices on **Trans-Amadi**, schools and hotels.

## What matters more than shape

1. Chemistry: **LiFePO4**
2. A good **BMS**
3. Matching your inverter's voltage
4. A written warranty

See [what a BMS is](/blog/what-is-a-bms-lithium-battery).

Filling stations along the **East-West Road**, in **Port Harcourt, Warri, Effurun, Ughelli and Yenagoa** run generators for long hours. Solar can carry much of that load.

## What a station runs

<div className="table-scroll">

| Area | Loads |
|---|---|
| Forecourt | Dispensers, canopy lights |
| Office | Computer, POS, printer, fan |
| Shop | Fridges, lights |
| Security | CCTV, perimeter lights |

</div>

## Where things go

- **Panels:** on the canopy or office roof, if strong enough
- **Inverter and batteries:** in a dedicated room **away from fuel vapour**
- **Cables:** in proper conduit, following fuel site rules

## Safety first

Fuel sites have strict rules. Equipment stays outside hazardous zones. Earthing and surge protection are a must. Read [earthing and surge protection](/blog/earthing-and-surge-protection-for-solar).

## Hybrid design

Solar by day, battery for the evening, generator as backup for very busy nights or long cloudy spells.

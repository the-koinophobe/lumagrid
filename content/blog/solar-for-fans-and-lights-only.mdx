Heat and mosquitoes. No light. No sleep. For many families, the first goal is simple: **fans and lights all night**.

## How much power

<div className="table-scroll">

| Item | Watts | How many | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ceiling / standing fan | 60W | 3 | 180W |
| LED bulb | 10W | 6 | 60W |
| Phone charging | 10W | 2 | 20W |
| **Total** | | | **About 260W** |

</div>

## How much battery

260W for 10 hours = **2.6kWh**.

A 5kWh lithium battery gives about 4.5kWh. That covers the night with room to spare.

## The system

- 3kVA hybrid inverter
- 5kWh lithium battery
- Four 600W panels
- From about **₦2.8M** installed

## Why not smaller?

You can go smaller. But a 3kVA inverter leaves room to add a TV, fridge or iron later. It saves you buying a new inverter. Read [start small and expand](/blog/start-small-and-expand-solar).

## Fan tips

- DC or "energy-saving" fans use less power.
- Ceiling fans move more air for the power.

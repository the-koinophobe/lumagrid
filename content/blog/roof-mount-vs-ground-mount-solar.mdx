Most solar panels in Nigeria sit on roofs. But some compounds have open land. Which is better?

## Side by side

<div className="table-scroll">

| | Roof mount | Ground mount |
|---|---|---|
| Space | Uses the roof | Needs open land |
| Cost | Lower | Higher (frame, concrete) |
| Theft risk | Lower | Higher |
| Cleaning | Harder | Easy |
| Best angle | Limited by roof | Free to choose |
| Floods | Safe | Must be raised |
| Roof leaks | Risk if badly done | None |

</div>

## Choose the roof if…

- Your roof gets sun most of the day.
- The roof is strong.
- You want lower cost and less theft risk.

Read [aluminium vs zinc roofs for solar](/blog/aluminium-vs-zinc-roof-for-solar).

## Choose the ground if…

- Your roof is shaded by trees or tall buildings.
- Your roof is old or weak, like some older houses in **Sapele** or **Old GRA**.
- You have secure open land, like many schools, churches and farms.

## Ground-mount must-haves

1. Strong galvanised or aluminium frame
2. Concrete footings
3. Fence and anti-theft bolts. See [panel theft prevention](/blog/solar-panel-theft-prevention).
4. In flood areas, panels high above the water line

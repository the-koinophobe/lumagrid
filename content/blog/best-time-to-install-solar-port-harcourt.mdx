"Should I wait for dry season?" Many people in **Port Harcourt, Warri and Yenagoa** ask this. Here is the honest answer.

## Dry season

About **November to February**.

- Roofs are dry. Work is easier.
- Panels produce well (with some harmattan haze).
- Busy season for installers, so book early.

## Rainy season

About **March or April to October**.

- Work happens in dry windows between showers.
- Everything is sealed against rain.
- You start saving fuel right away.

## What matters more than the month

<div className="table-scroll">

| Thing | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Sizing for rainy season | So the system keeps up from April to October |
| Good installer | Proper sealing and mounting |
| Starting sooner | Every month of waiting = more fuel bought |

</div>

## The cost of waiting

If you spend ₦300,000 a month on petrol, waiting four months for "dry season" costs about **₦1.2M**. That is a big part of a system.

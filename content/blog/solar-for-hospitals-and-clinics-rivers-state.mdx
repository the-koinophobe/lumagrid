For a clinic in **Port Harcourt, Obio-Akpor, Eleme or Bori**, a power cut is not just a cost. It can be a risk to patients. Solar with lithium storage gives steady power.

## Critical vs non-critical loads

We split the building into two circuits:

<div className="table-scroll">

| Critical (always on) | Non-critical |
|---|---|
| Vaccine and drug fridges | Office ACs |
| Theatre and delivery room lights | Water heaters |
| Oxygen concentrators | Kitchen |
| Lab equipment | Staff quarters |
| Computers and records | Outdoor lighting |

</div>

The critical circuit runs on solar and batteries at all times.

## No-break power

When the inverter powers the critical circuit all the time, there is **no gap** when NEPA goes. Equipment does not reboot.

## Fridges for drugs and vaccines

They must stay cold day and night. We size the battery so they never go warm, even on cloudy days, with grid or generator top-up if needed.

Clinics need good **earthing** and **surge protection**. Read [earthing and surge protection for solar](/blog/earthing-and-surge-protection-for-solar).

**10kVA** is for bigger homes and businesses: duplexes in **GRA Port Harcourt** and **Asaba**, offices on **Trans-Amadi**, guest houses in **Yenagoa**.

## The price

Roughly **₦5.1M to ₦6.5M** installed with:

- 10kVA pure sine wave hybrid inverter
- About 15kWh lithium
- About 6kW to 7kW of panels

## Rough breakdown

<div className="table-scroll">

| Part | Rough share |
|---|---|
| 15kWh lithium | About ₦3.3M |
| 10kVA inverter | About ₦1.1M |
| ~6.6kW panels | About ₦1.1M |
| Mounting, cables, protection, labour | About ₦250,000+ |
| **Total (middle)** | **About ₦5.8M** |

</div>

## Battery options

<div className="table-scroll">

| Battery | Good for |
|---|---|
| 10kWh | One AC at night, other loads |
| 15kWh | Two ACs part of the night |
| 20kWh+ | Two ACs all night, pump, freezers |

</div>

## Big buildings

Large offices and hotels may need **three-phase** systems or several inverters. These are designed individually.

Panels need light. So what happens when the sun goes down in **Port Harcourt, Warri or Yenagoa**?

## The day-night cycle

<div className="table-scroll">

| Time | Panels | Battery | Your home runs on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | Starting | Charging | Panels |
| Midday | Strong | Filling up | Panels |
| Evening | Stopping | Starting to drain | Battery |
| Night | Off | Draining | Battery |
| Low battery | Off | Near empty | Grid / generator (hybrid) |

</div>

## The battery is your night power

Everything you use after sunset comes from the battery. Lights, fans, TV, fridge, AC.

## How to have enough night power

1. **List your night loads.** Read [how to calculate your load](/blog/how-to-calculate-your-home-load-in-watts).
2. **Size the battery** for those hours. Read [how long your battery lasts](/blog/how-long-will-my-solar-battery-last-at-night).
3. **Size the panels** to refill it the next day, even in rainy season.

## Make the night cheaper

- Iron, wash and pump in the **day**.
- Use **LED** lights and **inverter ACs**.
- Set the AC to 24–25°C.

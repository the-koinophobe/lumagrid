Churches across **Port Harcourt, Obio-Akpor, Okrika and Bonny** spend a lot on diesel for Sunday services and vigils. Solar is a great fit for churches.

## Why churches suit solar

A church uses power on a few days, but the sun shines every day. The panels can **charge the batteries through the week**. On Sunday, the stored power runs the service.

## What a church runs

<div className="table-scroll">

| Item | Watts (typical) |
|---|---|
| Ceiling / wall fans (10) | 600W |
| LED lights (20) | 400W |
| PA system / mixer / amps | 500–2,000W |
| Projector + laptop | 300W |
| Instruments | 200–500W |

</div>

A mid-size church can use **2kW to 4kW** during service.

## Typical systems

<div className="table-scroll">

| Church size | Suggested system |
|---|---|
| Small (fans, lights, PA) | 5kVA–6kVA, 8kWh+ lithium |
| Medium hall | 10kVA, 15kWh+ lithium |
| Large hall with ACs | Custom, often hybrid with generator |

</div>

## Vigils and night programmes

At night, all power comes from the battery. We size the battery for your longest regular night event.

## Tips

- Use LED lights.
- Set sound system levels sensibly.
- Keep the generator as backup for crusades.

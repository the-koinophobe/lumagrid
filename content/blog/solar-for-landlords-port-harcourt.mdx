If you own flats or a lodge in **Port Harcourt, Rumuodara, Eneka, Rukpokwu or Choba**, you know the fight: who bought fuel, who used the generator, who did not pay. Solar ends it.

## How a shared system works

1. Panels on the roof.
2. One big inverter and lithium battery bank.
3. Each flat or room has its **own breaker** or **sub-meter**.
4. You set **limits**, so one tenant cannot drain it.

## What tenants can run

You decide. Common rules:

- Lights, fans, TV, phones, fridge: **yes**
- Pressing iron: **daytime only**
- ACs, hot plates: **no**, or only with their own sub-meter

## How landlords recover the cost

- A monthly **service charge**
- **Higher rent** for solar units
- **Sub-meters** so tenants pay for what they use
- Fewer empty rooms

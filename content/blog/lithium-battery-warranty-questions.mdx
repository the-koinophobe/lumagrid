A lithium battery is the most expensive part of your solar system. The warranty protects that money. But only if it is a real warranty.

## The 7 questions

### 1. How many years?

Look for **5 years or more**.

### 2. How many cycles?

A cycle is one full use and refill. Good LiFePO4 batteries are rated for thousands of cycles.

### 3. What capacity is promised?

A real warranty says something like: "at least 70% (or 80%) capacity after X years or Y cycles". Without this, a weak battery may still be "working".

### 4. Who handles claims in Nigeria?

If the maker is abroad, who checks the battery here? Who replaces it? Get a name and address.

### 5. What voids it?

Common reasons:

- Flood or water damage
- Physical damage
- Wrong inverter settings
- Opening the case
- Using it outside its limits

### 6. Is the installation covered?

Battery warranty covers the battery. **Workmanship warranty** covers the install. LumaGrid gives a 2-year workmanship warranty. Read [solar warranty explained](/blog/solar-warranty-explained).

### 7. Is it in writing?

"It has warranty" means nothing. You need a document with the serial number, date and seller details.

## What a salon runs

<div className="table-scroll">

| Item | Watts |
|---|---|
| Clippers (each) | 10–20W |
| Fans | 60W each |
| LED lights | 100W total |
| TV | 100W |
| Steriliser | 50–100W |
| Hand hair dryer | 1,000–2,000W |
| Hood dryer / steamer | 1,000–1,500W |

</div>

## The dryer rule

Dryers and steamers are the heavy loads. A 3kVA inverter can handle **one** at a time with the rest. A 5kVA handles **two**. Read [inverter overload: what to do](/blog/inverter-overload-what-to-do).

## Payback

Our [small business guide](/blog/solar-for-small-businesses-in-rivers-state) shows barber shops spending ₦165,000 to ₦240,000 a month on fuel. Solar often pays back in under a year.

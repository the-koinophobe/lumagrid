Most people in **Port Harcourt, Warri and Yenagoa** rent. "Is solar only for landlords?" No. Tenants can have solar too.

## Step 1: Ask the landlord

Ask in writing. Explain:

- Where the panels go
- That holes will be sealed
- That you will remove it and fix the roof when you leave

## Step 2: Agree on ownership

Write it down: **the system belongs to you**. That avoids fights when you move.

## Step 3: Plan for moving

<div className="table-scroll">

| Part | Easy to move? |
|---|---|
| Inverter | Yes |
| Lithium battery | Yes |
| Panels and rails | Yes, with a technician |
| Cables | Usually replaced |

</div>

## If the landlord says no

- A **portable power station** for fans, phones and laptops. Read [solar generator vs full system](/blog/solar-generator-vs-full-solar-system).
- A few panels on a **balcony** or in the yard.
- Ask about a **shared building system**. Read [solar for landlords](/blog/solar-for-landlords-port-harcourt).

You want cold drinks and safe food when NEPA goes. Should you buy a **DC solar fridge** or keep a **normal fridge** on your solar inverter?

## What is a DC solar fridge?

It runs on **DC power** straight from a battery. No inverter in between. That saves some power.

## What is a normal (AC) fridge?

It is the fridge you buy in any shop. It runs on 220V **AC** power. On solar, the inverter turns battery power into AC for it.

## When DC makes sense

- A shop with **only** a fridge and lights
- A remote site with a small panel and battery
- A farm or riverine site where every watt counts

## When a normal fridge makes sense

- You have a full home solar system already.
- You want a big double-door fridge.
- You want easy repairs.

Modern "inverter" fridges (with a variable-speed compressor) use much less power than old ones. They work very well on solar.

## Always use pure sine wave

A normal fridge on a modified sine wave inverter can run hot and fail early. Read [pure vs modified sine wave](/blog/pure-sine-wave-vs-modified-sine-wave).

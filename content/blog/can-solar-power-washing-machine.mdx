Laundry day is easier when you do not need the generator. Can solar handle a washing machine? Yes, with the right size.

## Two kinds of power use

1. **The motor** turns the drum. About 300W to 500W.
2. **The heater** warms the water. Can be 2,000W or more.

Most homes in Port Harcourt wash with cold water. That makes it easy on solar.

## What inverter you need

<div className="table-scroll">

| Wash type | 3kVA | 5kVA |
|---|---|---|
| Cold wash, twin tub | Yes | Yes |
| Cold wash, front-loader | Usually | Yes |
| Hot wash | Risky | Yes, with care |

</div>

## Best time to wash

Wash between **10am and 3pm**. The panels do the work. Your battery stays full for night.

## Tips

- Use **cold water** settings.
- Do not iron and microwave at the same time.
- Full loads are better than many small loads.

Once you add more than one AC, a pump or a business load, a 5kVA system starts to struggle. That is when people look at **10kVA**.

## Quick compare

<div className="table-scroll">

| | 5kVA | 10kVA |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | 2–3 bedroom home | Duplex, office, guest house |
| ACs | One small inverter AC | Two 1.5HP inverter ACs |
| Water pump | Small pump, daytime | 1HP pump |
| Battery | About 5kWh | About 15kWh or more |
| Panels | 5 to 6 × 600W | 10 to 14 × 600W |
| Price (2026) | About ₦2.2M–₦2.8M | About ₦5.1M–₦6.5M |

</div>

## Signs you need 10kVA

- You have **two or more ACs** you want on solar.
- You have a **borehole pump** and a freezer.
- You run an **office** with computers, printers and ACs.
- You run a **guest house** or small hotel.

## Signs 5kVA is enough

- One AC, used for a few hours.
- Normal family loads.
- You want to save money now.

## Battery is the real cost

A 10kVA inverter does not cost twice a 5kVA. The big jump is the **battery**. Running two ACs at night needs a lot of stored power. That is why 10kVA systems need 15kWh or more. Read [5kWh vs 10kWh battery](/blog/5kwh-vs-10kwh-solar-battery).

"Light don go again." In **Port Harcourt, Obio-Akpor, Eleme and Yenagoa**, you hear it every day. Here is why it happens and what you can do.

## Why light goes

<div className="table-scroll">

| Cause | What it means |
|---|---|
| Low supply | Not enough power reaching PHED to share |
| Load shedding | Areas take turns |
| Transformer overload | Too many homes on one transformer |
| Line faults | Broken lines, bad joints |
| Vandalism | Cables and parts stolen |
| Storms | Rainy-season damage |

</div>

## What you can do today

### 1. Report faults

Use PHED's official channels. Give your **account or meter number**, address and the fault. Keep the reference number.

### 2. Protect appliances

- Stabilisers for fridges and ACs
- Surge protection at the distribution board
- Unplug sensitive items in storms

### 3. Track your hours

If you are billed Band A but get far fewer hours, record them and complain. See [solar vs PHED Band A](/blog/solar-vs-phed-band-a-port-harcourt).

## Your backup options

<div className="table-scroll">

| Option | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|
| Petrol generator | Cheap to buy | Fuel every day, noise, fumes |
| Inverter only | Quiet | Needs long grid hours to charge |
| Hybrid solar | Free sun, uses PHED too | Higher start cost |

</div>

When you buy solar panels in Port Harcourt or Warri, a seller may ask: "Mono or poly?" Here is what that means.

## What the words mean

Solar panels are made of small squares called **cells**. Cells are made from silicon.

- **Mono** (monocrystalline) cells are cut from one pure crystal. They are black.
- **Poly** (polycrystalline) cells are made from many small crystals melted together. They are blue and speckled.

## Why efficiency matters on a Nigerian roof

Many roofs in Port Harcourt and Yenagoa have water tanks, parapets and odd shapes. Space is limited. Mono panels give you more power from each square metre. So you can fit your system on a smaller roof.

## Heat

Panels lose a little power when they get hot. Our sun is strong and our roofs get very hot. Mono panels lose slightly less in heat. Over 25 years, that adds up.

## Price

Today, mono panels are not much more expensive than poly. Because they make more power, mono often costs **less per watt**. If someone offers very cheap poly panels, ask why. They could be old stock or used. Read [new vs used (tokunbo) panels](/blog/new-vs-used-tokunbo-solar-panels).

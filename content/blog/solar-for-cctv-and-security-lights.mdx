Darkness invites trouble. When NEPA goes off at night, compounds in Port Harcourt, Warri and Yenagoa can go completely dark. Solar keeps cameras and lights on.

## How much power

<div className="table-scroll">

| Item | Watts |
|---|---|
| CCTV camera | 4–8W each |
| DVR / NVR recorder | 10–25W |
| LED security light | 10–30W each |
| Gate motor (standby) | Small, but higher when moving |

</div>

**Example:** 4 cameras (24W) + recorder (20W) + 4 LED lights (60W) = **about 104W**.

12 hours = **about 1.25kWh** every night.

## Two ways to do it

1. **On your home solar system.** Simple. Just make sure the battery covers it with your other night loads.
2. **A small separate system.** Keeps security on even if the home system trips.

## Tips

- Use LED lights with motion sensors to save power.
- Put the recorder on solar too, or cameras record nothing.
- Protect your panels too. Read [solar panel theft prevention](/blog/solar-panel-theft-prevention).

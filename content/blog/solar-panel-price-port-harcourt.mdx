"How much is one solar panel?" It is a common question in **Port Harcourt, Warri and Yenagoa**. The honest answer: it moves with the naira. But here is a useful guide.

## Our rough guide

In our installed systems, panels work out to about **₦170,000 per kW**.

<div className="table-scroll">

| Panel | Rough share of system price |
|---|---|
| 450W | About ₦75,000 |
| 550W | About ₦95,000 |
| 600W | About ₦100,000 |

</div>

These are planning figures, not shop prices.

## Price per watt matters most

```
Price ÷ Watts = Price per watt
```

A cheap 300W panel can cost **more per watt** than a 600W panel. It also takes more roof space.

## What changes panel prices

- **Exchange rate.** Most panels are imported.
- **Brand.** Tier-one brands like Jinko and Longi cost more.
- **Type.** N-type (TOPCon) costs a little more than older P-type. Read [N-type vs P-type](/blog/n-type-vs-p-type-solar-panels).
- **Condition.** New vs used. Read [new vs tokunbo panels](/blog/new-vs-used-tokunbo-solar-panels).

## Panels are not the whole system

Panels are only about a fifth of a typical system price. The battery is the biggest part. See [how much solar costs](/blog/how-much-does-solar-cost-in-port-harcourt).

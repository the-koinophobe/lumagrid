With a monitoring app, you can check your solar from anywhere: your office in **Trans-Amadi**, church in **Okrika**, or on a trip.

## What the app shows

<div className="table-scroll">

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PV (solar) watts | What the panels make now |
| Today's solar kWh | Total solar energy today |
| Battery % / SOC | How full the battery is |
| Load watts | What your home uses now |
| Grid watts | What you take from PHED or BEDC |

</div>

## Use it to catch problems

- **Solar kWh dropping** on sunny days? Clean panels or check shade.
- **Battery low at sunrise**? Night load too high or battery ageing.
- **Load spikes**? Someone ran the iron and kettle together.

Read [why your battery is not charging](/blog/why-is-my-solar-battery-not-charging).

## Remote help

Share access with your installer. We can see alarms and settings and often fix issues without a visit.

## Setup tips

1. Connect the inverter's Wi-Fi module to a stable router or MiFi.
2. Keep the router powered by solar.
3. Turn on alarm notifications.

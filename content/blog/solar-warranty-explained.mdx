"Does it have warranty?" Yes, but there is more than one. Here is what each covers.

## The four warranties

<div className="table-scroll">

| Warranty | Covers | Typical length |
|---|---|---|
| Panel product | Defects in the panel | Often 10–12 years |
| Panel performance | Power output over time | Often 25 years |
| Inverter | Inverter faults | Varies by brand |
| Battery | Battery faults and capacity | 5+ years for good lithium |
| Workmanship | The installation | LumaGrid: 2 years |

</div>

LumaGrid's trust bar says **10-year panel performance warranty** on what we install. Brands often give longer performance cover, as stated on their datasheets.

## Workmanship is the one people forget

If a cable comes loose, the roof leaks, or a breaker was wrong, that is **workmanship**. It is the installer's job to fix it.

## What voids warranties

- Floods or water damage
- Someone else opening or rewiring the system
- Wrong settings changed by the user
- Physical damage

## Keep these safe

1. Invoice
2. Serial numbers (photos of labels)
3. Installation photos
4. Warranty documents

Many homes, lodges and compounds in Port Harcourt, Warri and Yenagoa pump water every morning. Most use a generator. Let us compare it with solar.

## The generator pump

You start the generator. The pump runs for an hour. But people often leave the generator on for other things too. It burns fuel every day.

**Example:** a small generator burns 1 to 2 litres to fill the tank. At about ₦1,500 a litre, that is ₦1,500 to ₦3,000 a day. Over a month: about **₦45,000 to ₦90,000**, just for water.

## The solar pump

Two ways:

1. **On your home solar system.** The pump runs in daylight on your inverter. Needs about 5kVA for a 1HP pump.
2. **A dedicated solar pump.** Panels connect to a pump controller. No battery. The pump runs whenever the sun is up.

## The tank is the battery

With solar, store **water**, not electricity. A bigger overhead tank means you can pump only in the day and still have water at night and on cloudy days.

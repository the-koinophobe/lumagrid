Estates are growing fast in **Asaba (Okpanam, Cable Point), Eneka, Rukpokwu and Igwuruta**. Buyers ask one question first: "How is light here?" Solar is a strong answer.

## Two models

<div className="table-scroll">

| | One system per house | Shared estate mini-grid |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns it | Home owner | Estate company |
| Billing | None | Meters and billing needed |
| Simplicity | High | Lower |
| Best for | Most estates | Large managed estates |

</div>

## Plan it during building

It is much cheaper to:

- Leave a clear, unshaded roof area for panels
- Run a **conduit from roof to inverter room**
- Build a small, dry, ventilated **inverter room** (upstairs in flood areas)
- Wire critical circuits separately

## Shared estate loads

- **Solar street lights**
- **Gatehouse** lights and CCTV
- **Estate borehole** on a solar pump

## Selling value

Homes with solar and steady water let and sell faster. In Asaba, buyers compare estates on power first.

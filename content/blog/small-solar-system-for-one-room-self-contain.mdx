Not everyone needs a big system. If you live in a self-contain in **Rumuokoro**, a student room in **Choba**, or a single room in **Abraka**, a small setup can change your life.

## What one room uses

<div className="table-scroll">

| Item | Watts |
|---|---|
| Fan | 50–70W |
| 3 LED bulbs | 30W |
| Phone + power bank | 20W |
| Laptop | 60W |
| Small TV | 40W |
| **Total** | **About 200–220W** |

</div>

10 hours at night = about **2kWh**.

## Options

<div className="table-scroll">

| Option | Good for |
|---|---|
| Portable power station | Students, renters, moving often |
| Small 1kVA–1.5kVA system + 1–2kWh lithium | Self-contain, fixed home |
| Share a lodge system | Many rooms in one building |

</div>

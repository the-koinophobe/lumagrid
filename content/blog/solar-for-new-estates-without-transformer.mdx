New estates in **Eneka, Rukpokwu, Igwuruta, Elelenwo, Okpanam and parts of Asaba** often go up before the grid. Some wait years for a transformer. Meanwhile, families run generators.

## Plan for off-grid now

With no grid, solar does all the work. So:

- **More panels** to refill the battery even on cloudy days
- **More battery** for the night
- A small generator as backup for long rainy spells (optional)

## Buy a hybrid inverter

A hybrid inverter works off-grid today. When PHED or BEDC finally connects, it uses the grid too. You do not replace anything.

You may have seen "solar generators" online. They are boxes with sockets and a handle. Some come with fold-up panels. Are they the same as a solar system? Not quite.

## What a solar generator is

It is a **battery**, a **small inverter** and **charging parts** in one box. People also call it a **power station**. You charge it from the wall, a car or small panels.

## What a full solar system is

Panels on your roof. A hybrid inverter on the wall. A lithium battery bank. It is wired into your home's sockets.

## Good uses for a power station

- Students in lodges near **Choba** or **Abraka**
- Laptop and Wi-Fi for remote work during outages
- Travel, church events and outdoor work
- Backup for a single room

## When you need a full system

- You want the generator off for good.
- You run a fridge, freezer or AC.
- You have a family home or shop.

## The money question

Power stations often cost **more per kWh** of storage than a full system. They are convenient, not cheap. If you need lots of power, a full system is better value.

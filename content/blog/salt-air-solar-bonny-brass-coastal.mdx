Living by the sea on **Bonny Island**, **Brass**, **Okrika** or in other riverine towns means salt in the air. Salt is hard on metal and electronics.

## What salt does

<div className="table-scroll">

| Part | Risk | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Rails and bolts | Rust | Aluminium rails, stainless steel bolts |
| Cable connectors | Corrosion | Quality sealed connectors, checked yearly |
| Junction boxes | Water and salt inside | Sealed, rated boxes |
| Panels | Salt film | Fresh-water rinse |
| Inverter | Corroded boards | Install indoors |

</div>

## Build it right

1. **Aluminium** rails, **stainless** fasteners.
2. Sealed DC isolators and junction boxes.
3. Inverter and batteries **indoors**, away from spray.
4. Cable entries sealed.

## Look after it

- Rinse panels with **fresh water** every few weeks.
- Check connectors and bolts once a year.
- Look for white crust or rust and act early.

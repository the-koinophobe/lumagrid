The **3kVA** system is the most popular starter system in **Port Harcourt, Warri and Yenagoa**. Here is what it costs and what you get.

## The price

**From about ₦1.7M**, fully installed.

## What is included

<div className="table-scroll">

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Inverter | 3kVA pure sine wave hybrid |
| Battery | 3.2kWh lithium |
| Panels | 4 × 600W |
| Mounting | Rails and clamps |
| Protection | Breakers, surge protection, earthing |
| Labour | Installation and testing |
| Warranty | 2-year workmanship |

</div>

## What it runs

- 4 to 6 LED lights
- 2 to 3 fans
- TV and decoder
- Phones, laptop, Wi-Fi
- A small fridge

About 7 hours of a 400W evening load from the battery.

## What it does not run

A 1.5HP AC. For that, see [3kVA vs 5kVA](/blog/3kva-vs-5kva-solar-system).

## What raises the price

- More battery for longer nights
- More panels
- Upstairs buildings with long cable runs
- Sites far from Port Harcourt or reached by boat

## Typical systems

<div className="table-scroll">

| Business | Suggested system |

| 1 freezer + lights | 3kVA, 3.2kWh lithium, 4 panels |
| 2 freezers + lights + fan | 3kVA–5kVA, 5kWh lithium, 5–6 panels |

## Payback example

A shop runs a petrol generator 8 hours a day for its freezers. At about ₦1,250 per litre, that can be ₦300,000 a month. A ₦3M system pays back in about 10 months.

## Tips

1. Keep freezers full.
2. Keep them in the shade.
3. Check door seals.
4. Replace very old freezers.

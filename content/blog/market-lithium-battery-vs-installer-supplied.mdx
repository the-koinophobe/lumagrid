You can buy a lithium battery in many markets and online. You can also get one from your solar installer. Which is better?

## What can go wrong with a market battery

- **Fake capacity.** The label says 10kWh. The battery holds much less.
- **Weak cells.** Grade B or used cells inside a new box. Read [Grade A vs Grade B cells](/blog/grade-a-vs-grade-b-lithium-cells).
- **Weak BMS.** Poor protection inside.
- **No local warranty.** The seller disappears or says "no refund".
- **Wrong match.** It does not work well with your inverter.

## Settings matter

Lithium needs the right charge voltage, cut-off and current. Wrong settings shorten its life. A good installer sets these and tests them.

## If you buy it yourself

1. Get the datasheet.
2. Check it is **LiFePO4**.
3. Ask for **kWh**, not just Ah.
4. Ask for a **written warranty** with the seller's address.
5. Check it works with your inverter model.

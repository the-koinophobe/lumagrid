When light goes, many homes in Port Harcourt reach for rechargeable fans and lamps. They help. But are they cheaper than solar in the long run?

## What rechargeables do well

- They are cheap to buy.
- No installer needed.
- Good for a few hours.

## Where they fall short

1. **They need light to charge.** If NEPA does not bring light, they stay empty.
2. **Batteries wear out.** Most die in 1 to 2 years.
3. **They are small.** One fan, one room.
4. **No fridge, no TV.**

## A family example

A family buys 3 rechargeable fans and 4 lamps. They replace them often. They still run a generator for the TV and fridge. Over a few years, that adds up to a lot.

A 3kVA system runs all of it from the sun. No fuel. No buying fans again.

Solar equipment is valuable. In busy areas of **Port Harcourt, Warri, Yenagoa** and in lodges and compounds, theft is a real worry. These steps help.

## Protect the panels

1. **Anti-theft bolts** on every clamp.
2. Keep panels **on the roof** where possible. Ground mounts need fencing. Read [roof vs ground mount](/blog/roof-mount-vs-ground-mount-solar).
3. **Security lights** that come on at night.
4. **CCTV** facing the roof or array. See [solar for CCTV](/blog/solar-for-cctv-and-security-lights).

## Protect the batteries and inverter

Batteries are the most valuable part.

- A **lockable room** with a strong door
- Burglar bars on windows
- Wall brackets bolted from inside
- Out of sight from the street

## Record everything

<div className="table-scroll">

| Item | What to record |
|---|---|
| Panels | Serial numbers (photo of each label) |
| Battery | Serial number, capacity |
| Inverter | Model and serial number |
| Invoice | Keep it safe |

</div>

## Lodges and shared buildings

Lock the battery room. Only the landlord or caretaker has the key. Read [solar for landlords](/blog/solar-for-landlords-port-harcourt).

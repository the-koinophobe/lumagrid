Offices in **Trans-Amadi, Woji, GRA, Peter Odili Road and Rumuola** lose work every time power drops. Computers restart. Internet goes. Staff wait for the generator. Solar stops that.

## Why offices suit solar

Most offices work from about 8am to 5pm. That is exactly when panels produce. The battery covers early evenings and cloudy hours.

## No-break power for servers

Set the inverter so it powers critical loads all the time. Then there is no gap when PHED goes off.

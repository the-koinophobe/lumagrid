Solar quotes are full of letters. Here is what they mean, in plain words.

## The water tank picture

Think of power like water:

- **kW / kVA** = how wide the tap is (how much flows at once)
- **kWh** = how much water is in the tank (how much you have in total)

## The words

<div className="table-scroll">

| Word | Means | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| W (watt) | Power at one moment | Appliances, panels | Fan = 60W, panel = 600W |
| kW | 1,000 watts | Loads, panel arrays | 6 panels × 600W = 3.6kW |
| kVA | Inverter size | Inverters | 5kVA inverter |
| Wh / kWh | Energy over time | Batteries, daily use | 5kWh battery |
| Ah | Battery charge | Batteries | 100Ah |
| V | Voltage | Batteries, systems | 48V system |

</div>

## kVA vs kW

kVA is a little bigger than kW for most inverters. A **5kVA** inverter usually gives about **4kW to 5kW** of real power. Check the datasheet.

## Ah to kWh

```
Ah × Volts ÷ 1,000 = kWh
```

- 51.2V × 100Ah ÷ 1,000 = **5.1kWh**
- 12V × 200Ah ÷ 1,000 = **2.4kWh**

That is why a "200Ah battery" can mean very different things. Always ask for **kWh**.

## Simple kWh examples

- A 100W fan for 10 hours = **1kWh**
- A 1,000W iron for 30 minutes = **0.5kWh**

## Why it matters when buying

- Want to run an AC? Look at **kVA**.
- Want power all night? Look at **kWh**.

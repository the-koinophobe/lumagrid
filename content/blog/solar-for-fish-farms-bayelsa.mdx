Catfish and tilapia farms around **Yenagoa, Kaiama, Odi and Sagbama** need water moving and oxygen up. Diesel pumps and long fuel trips eat profits. Solar helps.

## What a fish farm runs

<div className="table-scroll">

| Load | When | Battery needed? |
|---|---|---|
| Water pump (pond changes) | Day | No (solar pump) |
| Aerator | Often night | Yes |
| Security lights | Night | Yes |
| Freezer for harvest | 24 hours | Yes |

</div>

## Day pumping, no battery

A **solar pump** runs straight from panels. It changes water while the sun shines. No fuel. No battery.

## Night aeration

Oxygen in ponds often drops at night. Aerators may need to run then. Size the battery for the aerator's watts × night hours.

## Floods

Many farms are low-lying. Raise panels on strong frames. Keep inverters and batteries in a raised, locked hut. Read [solar in flood-prone areas](/blog/protect-solar-system-from-flooding-bayelsa).

## Smoking kilns

Fish smoking kilns use wood or charcoal for heat. Small fans for airflow can run on solar.

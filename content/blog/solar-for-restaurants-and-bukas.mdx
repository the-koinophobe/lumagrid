Bukas and eateries in **Port Harcourt, Warri, Effurun and Yenagoa** run on cold storage and comfort. Customers stay where it is cool and bright.

## What a food spot runs

<div className="table-scroll">

| Load | Watts |
|---|---|
| Chest freezers (2) | 200–400W running |
| Drinks fridge | 150W |
| Fans (4) | 240W |
| LED lights | 100W |
| TV | 100W |
| Blender | 1,000–2,000W (bursts) |
| POS / phone | 20W |

</div>

## Suggested system

- 5kVA hybrid inverter
- 5kWh to 8kWh lithium (for freezers at night)
- 6 to 8 × 600W panels

## Keep cooking on gas

Electric stoves use huge power for a long time. Gas is cheaper.

## Save more

- Blend in the daytime.
- Keep freezers full and closed.
- Use LED lights.

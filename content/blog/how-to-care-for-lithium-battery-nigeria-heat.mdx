Lithium batteries do not need water. They do not need cleaning. But they do not like **heat**. In Port Harcourt, Warri and Yenagoa, heat is the main thing that shortens battery life.

## Pick the right room

Good spots:

- An inside room that stays cool
- A store with a window or vent
- A wall that does not face the afternoon sun

Bad spots:

- A hot zinc-roof store with no air
- Next to a generator or cooker
- In direct sun
- A ground floor that floods

## Keep air moving

Leave space around the battery. Do not pack boxes on it. A small vent or fan helps in very hot rooms.

## Keep it clean and clear

- No petrol, diesel or paint in the battery room
- No clothes or cartons on top
- No water leaks above it

## Good charging habits

1. Let the panels fill it every day.
2. Avoid draining it to zero often.
3. Keep the inverter's lithium settings correct.
4. If you have a hybrid inverter, let grid power top it up on very cloudy days.

## Monthly check

<div className="table-scroll">

| Check | What is normal |
|---|---|
| Alarm lights | None |
| Touch the case | Warm is okay, very hot is not |
| Shape | Flat sides, no bulging |
| Smell | None |
| Night backup | Same as before |

</div>

If something is wrong, switch off at the breaker and call your installer.

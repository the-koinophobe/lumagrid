**5kVA** is the step up for families who want a washing machine, ironing freedom or one small AC.

## The price

Roughly **₦2.2M to ₦2.8M** installed with:

- 5kVA pure sine wave hybrid inverter
- 5kWh lithium battery
- About 3kW of panels (5 to 6 × 600W)

## What changes the price

- **More battery.** 10kWh adds about ₦1.1M.
- **Building type.** Upstairs buildings may need longer cables.
- **Location.** Boat or long-distance sites add transport.

## How to keep the cost down

1. Use an **inverter AC**. It needs less battery.
2. Run heavy loads **in the day**.
3. Start with 5kWh and **add battery later**. Read [start small and expand](/blog/start-small-and-expand-solar).

You agree a price. Then the extras start. "Cable is extra." "Breaker is extra." Here is how to avoid that.

## The usual surprises

<div className="table-scroll">

| Hidden cost | What it is |
|---|---|
| Cables | Thick DC and AC cable, especially on [upstairs buildings](/blog/solar-on-upstairs-building) |
| Protection | DC and AC breakers, fuses, surge protection |
| Earthing | Earth rod and cable |
| Mounting | Rails, clamps, roof hooks |
| Roof repairs | Weak timber or rusty sheets |
| Transport | Long trips or boats (Bonny, Brass) |
| Battery stand | Wall bracket or raised base |
| More battery | When the first one runs out too early |

</div>

## How to avoid them

1. Ask for an **itemised** quote.
2. Ask: **"Is this the full price, installed?"**
3. Check that protection and earthing are listed.
4. Size the battery right the first time.
5. Get transport costs in writing.

## Running costs to expect

- Panel cleaning. Read [how to clean panels](/blog/how-to-clean-solar-panels-safely).
- A yearly connection and settings check.
- Lead-acid replacement (avoid with lithium).

You work online. NEPA takes light. Your generator is loud and the neighbours' are louder. Solar fixes all of that.

## What a home office uses

<div className="table-scroll">

| Item | Watts |
|---|---|
| Laptop | 45–90W |
| Monitor | 20–40W |
| Wi-Fi router / MiFi | 10W |
| Standing fan | 50–70W |
| LED lights (3) | 30W |
| Phone charging | 10–20W |
| **Total** | **About 200–270W** |

</div>

## Day and night

In the day, panels cover your load. The battery only helps if a cloud passes. At night, the battery keeps your fan and Wi-Fi on.

## Suggested system

- 3kVA hybrid inverter
- 3.2kWh lithium battery
- Four 600W panels

From about **₦1.7M** installed. See [3kVA solar price](/blog/3kva-solar-system-price-nigeria).

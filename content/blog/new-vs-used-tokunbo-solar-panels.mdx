In markets across Nigeria you will find "fairly used" or **tokunbo** solar panels. They are cheaper. Are they a good deal?

## What you do not know

- How old is the panel?
- How much power has it lost?
- Was it dropped or cracked?
- Does it have hot spots?

You cannot see most of this.

## Hidden problems

- **Micro-cracks.** Tiny cracks you cannot see. They cut power over time.
- **Hot spots.** Burnt patches that waste power and can damage the panel.
- **Browning.** The clear layer turns yellow or brown. Less light gets in.
- **Water inside.** Bubbles or fog under the glass.

## Cost per watt matters

A used 250W panel at half price can still cost more **per watt** than a new 600W panel. And it takes more roof space.

## When used panels can work

- You can test each panel in full sun.
- The price is very low.
- It is for a small, non-critical setup.

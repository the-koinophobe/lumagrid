## Why prices differ between sellers

- **Cell grade.** Grade A costs more. Read [Grade A vs Grade B](/blog/grade-a-vs-grade-b-lithium-cells).
- **Real capacity.** Some labels overstate it. Read [how to spot a fake lithium battery](/blog/how-to-spot-fake-lithium-battery).
- **BMS quality.**
- **Warranty and support.**
- **Exchange rate** on the day.

## Always compare by kWh

```
Volts × Ah ÷ 1,000 = kWh
```

A "200Ah" 12V battery is only 2.4kWh. A "100Ah" 48V battery is about 5kWh.

## Cost per year

Lithium lasts 8 to 15 years. Lead-acid lasts about 2. Lithium usually costs **less per year**. Read [why lithium is better](/blog/why-lithium-ion-batteries-are-better-for-solar).

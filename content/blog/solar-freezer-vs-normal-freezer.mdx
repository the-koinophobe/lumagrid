Cold drinks, fish and meat sellers in Port Harcourt, Warri and Yenagoa lose money when freezers go warm. Solar fixes that. But which freezer is best?

## Freezers are special

Lights and fans go off when you sleep. A freezer **never** stops. It runs on and off all night. So the battery matters a lot.

## Simple battery guide

<div className="table-scroll">

| Freezers | Night battery (usable) |
|---|---|
| 1 freezer | About 1kWh |
| 2 freezers | About 2kWh |
| 4 freezers | About 4kWh |

Then add lights, fans and anything else you run at night.

## Tips to save power

1. Keep freezers **full**. Full freezers hold cold better.
2. Do not open them too often.
3. Keep them out of direct sun.
4. Check the door rubber. Bad seals waste power.
5. Replace very old freezers. They use far more power.

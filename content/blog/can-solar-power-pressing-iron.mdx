Ironing clothes is one of the most common questions we get. "Will my solar carry pressing iron?" Here is the simple answer.

## How much power an iron uses

Look at the label under the iron. Most dry irons in Nigeria say **1,000W to 1,200W**. Steam irons can be **2,000W or more**.

The iron heats, then clicks off, then heats again. So it does not use full power all the time.

## What inverter you need

<div className="table-scroll">

| Inverter | Ironing? |
|---|---|
| 1.5kVA–2kVA | Risky. Iron alone may trip it. |
| 3kVA | Yes, with little else on. |
| 5kVA | Yes, with fridge, fans and TV on. |

</div>

## Best time to iron

Iron between **10am and 3pm**. The sun is strong. Panels make most of the power. The battery stays full for the night.

## How much battery it uses

30 minutes of ironing ≈ **0.3 to 0.5kWh**. At night, that comes from your battery. In the day, panels cover it.

## Other heavy items

Kettles, microwaves and hair dryers are similar. Do not run them all at once. Read [can solar power a microwave and kettle](/blog/can-solar-power-microwave-and-kettle).

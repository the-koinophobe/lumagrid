Guest houses and hotels in **Yenagoa, Amassoma, Ogbia and Kaiama** run generators most of the day. Diesel is the biggest cost after staff. Solar can cut it sharply.

## Where hotel power goes

<div className="table-scroll">

| Area | Typical loads |
|---|---|
| Rooms | AC, fan, lights, TV, fridge |
| Reception | Lights, computer, POS, Wi-Fi |
| Kitchen and bar | Freezers, fridges, blender |
| Outside | Security lights, CCTV |
| Water | Borehole pump |

</div>

## The AC challenge

Ten rooms with 1.5HP ACs running at night is a lot of battery. That is why most hotels use **hybrid**:

- Solar and batteries carry lights, fans, TVs, fridges, freezers, CCTV and pumping.
- Some ACs run on solar.
- The generator covers full-house nights.

Read [solar vs diesel for businesses](/blog/solar-vs-diesel-generator-for-business).

## Room-by-room design

We list each room's loads, then size day and night separately. Simple changes help:

- Inverter ACs set at 24°C
- Key-card switches that cut power when guests leave
- LED lights everywhere

## Flood-safe install

Parts of Yenagoa flood. We mount inverters and batteries high or upstairs. Read [solar in flood-prone areas](/blog/protect-solar-system-from-flooding-bayelsa).

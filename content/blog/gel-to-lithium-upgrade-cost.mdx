If your gel or tubular batteries keep dying, you are not alone. Many homes in Port Harcourt, Warri and Ughelli upgrade to lithium. Here is what it costs and what changes.

## What you pay for

1. **The lithium battery.** The biggest part.
2. **Settings.** The inverter must be set for lithium.
3. **Cables and breaker.** Sometimes new ones are needed.
4. **A new inverter**, only if yours cannot handle lithium.

## Rough battery costs

Lithium storage costs about **₦220,000 per kWh** in our installed systems.

## Can your inverter stay?

We check:

- **Voltage.** A 48V inverter needs a 48V lithium battery.
- **Lithium setting.** Does it have a lithium or "user" charge mode?
- **Sine wave.** Is it pure sine wave?
- **Solar input.** Does it have MPPT for panels?

Many hybrid inverters bought in the last few years can stay. Old inverters often cannot.

## What you gain

<div className="table-scroll">

| | Before (gel / tubular) | After (lithium) |
|---|---|---|
| Usable power | About half | Most of it |
| Life | 1.5 to 3 years | 8 to 15 years |
| Water / care | Tubular: yes | None |
| Space | Big and heavy | Smaller, often wall-mounted |

</div>

## How to size the new battery

Do not just copy the old battery's Ah. Lithium gives more usable power. A 5kWh lithium often does the job of 8 to 10kWh of lead-acid.

Clean panels make more power. Here is how to clean them without damage or danger.

## When to clean

- **Early morning** or **late afternoon**, when panels are cool
- Every 4 to 6 weeks in dry season
- After harmattan dust or heavy soot

## What you need

- Clean water (a bucket or hose)
- A soft brush or sponge on a long pole
- Mild soap (only if needed)
- A soft squeegee (optional)

## Step by step

1. **Switch off** the inverter's solar input if your installer showed you how.
2. **Rinse** with clean water to loosen dirt.
3. **Gently brush** from top to bottom.
4. **Rinse again.** Leave no soap.
5. **Check** the inverter's solar output the next sunny day.

## Never do this

<div className="table-scroll">

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| Stand or kneel on panels | Cracks cells |
| Use sand, powder or steel wool | Scratches glass |
| Use a pressure washer | Can damage seals |
| Clean hot panels at midday | Thermal shock |
| Climb an unsafe roof | Falls are deadly |

</div>

## Upstairs and steep roofs

On a [storey building](/blog/solar-on-upstairs-building), leave it to people with harnesses and proper ladders.

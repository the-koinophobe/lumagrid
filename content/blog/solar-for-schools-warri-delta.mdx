Private schools in **Warri, Effurun, Asaba, Sapele and Ughelli** spend heavily on diesel. Solar fits schools better than almost any other building.

## Why schools are perfect for solar

School hours are **sun hours**. Panels feed classrooms directly. The battery only covers cloudy moments and after-school activities.

## What a school runs

<div className="table-scroll">

| Area | Loads |
|---|---|
| Classrooms | Fans, lights |
| Computer lab | Desktops or laptops, projector |
| Office | Printer, photocopier, computers |
| Security | CCTV, gate lights |
| Water | Borehole pump |

</div>

## Typical sizes

<div className="table-scroll">

| School | Suggested system |
|---|---|
| Small (6 classrooms, office) | 5kVA–6kVA, 5kWh+ lithium |
| Medium with computer lab | 10kVA, 10kWh+ lithium |
| Boarding school | Custom; extra battery for hostels |

</div>

## Borehole on solar

Pump water during school hours. Fill the tank by day. See [solar borehole guide](/blog/solar-borehole-water-pump-nigeria).

## A lesson in itself

Many schools use their solar system to teach science. Pupils can watch the inverter screen show power from the sun.

Business owners in **Port Harcourt, Warri, Effurun and Yenagoa** ask one thing: **"When will I get my money back?"**

## The formula

```
Payback (months) = System cost ÷ Monthly savings
```

## Step 1: Monthly savings

<div className="table-scroll">

| Saving | How to work it out |
|---|---|
| Fuel | Litres per day × price × 30 |
| Generator servicing | Yearly cost ÷ 12 |
| Grid units | Units replaced × your real price per unit |
| Lost sales | Your estimate per outage day |

</div>

## Step 2: Example

A shop burns 8 litres of petrol a day at about ₦1,250.

- Fuel: 8 × 1,250 × 30 = **₦300,000**
- Servicing: ₦180,000 ÷ 12 = **₦15,000**
- Total: **₦315,000 a month**

System cost: ₦3.8M.

```
3,800,000 ÷ 315,000 ≈ 12 months
```

## After payback

Panels last 25+ years. Lithium lasts 8 to 15 years. After payback, most of that money stays in your business.

## Things that speed payback

- Running heavy loads in the day
- Inverter ACs and LED lights
- A hybrid inverter that also uses grid power

The inverter size (5kVA) does not decide your battery. **Your night use does.** But here are the common setups.

## First: the voltage

Most 5kVA inverters are **48V**. Your battery bank must be 48V too.

## Lithium setups (most common now)

<div className="table-scroll">

| Lithium | Energy | Usable | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 × 48V 100Ah | ~5kWh | ~4.5kWh | Lights, fans, TV, fridge all night |
| 2 × 48V 100Ah | ~10kWh | ~8.5kWh | Add an AC for most of the night |
| 1 × 48V 200Ah | ~10kWh | ~8.5kWh | Same as above, one unit |

</div>

## Lead-acid setups (older style)

12V batteries must be joined **four in series** to make 48V.

<div className="table-scroll">

| Lead-acid | Energy | Usable (~50%) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 × 12V 200Ah | ~9.6kWh | ~4.8kWh |
| 8 × 12V 200Ah | ~19.2kWh | ~9.6kWh |

</div>

Eight heavy batteries take a lot of space and die in about 2 years. One or two lithium units do the same job for many years. Read [lithium vs tubular](/blog/lithium-vs-tubular-battery).

## Quick guide

- **No AC at night:** 5kWh lithium
- **AC for a few hours:** 5kWh to 8kWh lithium
- **AC most of the night:** 8kWh to 10kWh lithium

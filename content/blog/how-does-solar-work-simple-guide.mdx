Solar can seem like magic. It is not. It is three main parts working together.

## The three parts

<div className="table-scroll">

| Part | Job | Simple picture |
|---|---|---|
| Solar panels | Turn sunlight into electricity | The farm |
| Inverter | Makes power usable and manages it | The kitchen |
| Battery | Stores power for later | The store room |

</div>

## A day with solar

### Morning

The sun rises. Panels start making power. The inverter sends it to your fridge, fans and TV.

### Midday

The sun is strong. Panels make more than you use. The extra goes into the **battery**.

### Evening

The sun sets. Panels stop. The inverter now runs your home from the **battery**.

### Night

The battery keeps going until morning, if it is big enough. Read [how long your battery lasts](/blog/how-long-will-my-solar-battery-last-at-night).

### Cloudy days

Panels make less. If you have a **hybrid** inverter, it can top up from PHED, BEDC or a generator. Read [hybrid vs off-grid](/blog/hybrid-vs-off-grid-solar-nigeria).

## DC and AC

Panels and batteries make **DC** power. Your sockets use **AC**. The inverter changes DC to AC.

## What protects it all

- **Breakers and fuses** stop overloads
- **Surge protection** guards against lightning
- **Earthing** keeps you safe

Read [earthing and surge protection](/blog/earthing-and-surge-protection-for-solar).

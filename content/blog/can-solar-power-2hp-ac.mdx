Big living rooms and master bedrooms in Asaba, GRA Port Harcourt and Yenagoa often have a **2HP AC**. Can solar run it? Yes, with a bigger system.

## How much power a 2HP AC uses

- **Running hard:** about 1.5kW to 2kW
- **Inverter AC once cool:** often about 1kW to 1.3kW on average
- **Start-up:** a normal AC needs a big surge; an inverter AC starts gently

## System guide

<div className="table-scroll">

| Part | Size for one 2HP inverter AC + home loads |
|---|---|
| Inverter | 8kVA to 10kVA pure sine wave hybrid |
| Panels | About 5kW to 6kW (9 to 10 × 600W) |
| Battery | About 10kWh to 12kWh usable for 8 hours |

</div>

## Cost guide

This is close to our 10kVA range: roughly **₦5.1M to ₦6.5M** with 15kWh of lithium. Smaller batteries cost less, but the AC will not last the night. See [5kVA vs 10kVA](/blog/5kva-vs-10kva-solar-system).

## Ways to cut the cost

1. Use an **inverter-type** AC. Read [inverter AC vs normal AC](/blog/inverter-ac-vs-normal-ac-on-solar).
2. Set it at **24–25°C**.
3. Run it for part of the night, then use a fan.
4. Insulate the ceiling and use thick curtains.
